A focus timer that learns how you actually work, and a habit tracker that holds you to your word.

Check in with your mood before a session, get a session length that fits it, and let the app get sharper with every session it sees.

WHAT IT DOES

* Focus sessions with a pixel dial you can turn mid-session
* Habits with streaks, flexible schedules and one honest way out
* A mood log, and recommendations that read it
* Statistics: a heatmap of your days, focus time, mood against results
* An optional RPG layer — a map, opponents your sessions wear down, a character that grows with them. It can be switched off at first run and in Settings; the tracker underneath is the same either way.
* Brakes against burnout: the app will suggest stopping before you run yourself down

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

* No account. Nothing to register, nothing to log in to.
* No analytics, no ad SDKs, no background "events".
* No data leaves the device. The database lives on your phone; the network is used for update checks and nothing else.
* No subscription, no ads, no popup asking for a rating.

Free software under the GNU AGPL v3. The source is open, and the builds come from public CI on that same code.
